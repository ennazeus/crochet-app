# My Crochet App 🧶

A simple web app for collecting crochet patterns and keeping track of your progress.

I built this app to avoid losing my place in patterns and row counting — and to make crocheting a little calmer and more enjoyable.

---

## ✨ Features

- Save and organise your crochet patterns
- Keep track of rows and progress
- Add notes for each section
- Works offline
- No login required
- All data is stored locally on your device

---

## 💾 How it works

The app runs entirely in your browser.  
Your patterns and progress are saved locally on your device using local storage.

This means:
- No accounts
- No cloud sync
- No data collection
- No tracking
